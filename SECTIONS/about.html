ABOUT UP CEBU TTBDO

Pioneering Knowledge Translation & Enterprise in Central Visayas

The Technology Transfer and Business Development Office (TTBDO) of the University of the Philippines Cebu is the university's institutional engine for intellectual property management, commercialization, and entrepreneurial incubation. Operating under the tripartite mandate of the national university—teaching, research, and public service—we bridge academic discovery with regional industry needs to produce tangible economic and community impact.

OUR ASPIRATION

Our Vision

To be the premier university technology transfer and innovation powerhouse in Central Visayas, recognized for ethically stewarding intellectual creations, nurturing regional startups, and accelerating solutions that advance national development.

OUR COMMITMENT

Our Mission

To empower creators within the UP Cebu academic community by protecting their intellectual property, bridging university research with industry and public sectors, and cultivating an enduring culture of social enterprise and responsible commercialization.

Our Four Strategic Pillars

01

IP Governance & Stewardship

Ensuring robust legal protection for inventions, copyrights, and designs under Republic Act 10055 and UP System policies.

02

Commercial Licensing

Facilitating fair, mutually beneficial agreements with private and public organizations to translate university IP into market solutions.

03

Startup Acceleration

Supplying workspace, seed funding connections, and corporate structuring to launch successful student and faculty spin-offs.

04

Regional Extension

Empowering micro-enterprises and grassroots cooperatives across Region VII through accessible technology adoption and IP training.
